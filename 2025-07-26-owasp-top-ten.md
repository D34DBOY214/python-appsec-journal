# What I think the OWASP Top 10 means right now

Looking over the OWASP Top 10 list right now, it's clear that application security covers a wide variety of risks—ranging from access control issues and configuration mistakes to software supply chain problems and injection flaws. Even though a lot of the specific terminology is new to me, the main takeaway is that these categories represent the most critical security weaknesses that developers need to watch out for to keep applications safe.
